REACH is a contact storage and sharing platform. Put your details, your story and what you offer in one place, and share it with a link or a QR code.

Why REACH exists

Most of us are spread across many apps. One person asks for your WhatsApp, another for your LinkedIn, someone else wants your phone number, your address or a look at what you sell. You end up typing the same details again and again, and people still lose them.

REACH fixes that. You build a card once, with every platform and detail you want to share, and you can show what you do or sell. Then you share a single link or a QR code. Anyone can open it on any phone, with no app and no sign-up, and save your details straight to their contacts.
