REACH is a contact storage and sharing platform that puts all your details in one place, so people can connect with you in a single tap or scan.

Why REACH exists

Most of us are spread across many apps. One person asks for your WhatsApp, another for your LinkedIn, someone else wants your phone number or your office address. You end up typing the same details again and again, and people still lose them.

REACH fixes that. You build a card once, with every platform and detail you want to share. Then you share a single link or a QR code. Anyone can open it on any phone, with no app and no sign-up, and save your details straight to their contacts.

Students and graduates, freelancers, creatives, entrepreneurs, sales teams, event organisers, and anyone who meets new people and wants to be easy to reach. Whether you are handing over a business card or just chatting at an event, REACH makes sharing your details quick and tidy.
