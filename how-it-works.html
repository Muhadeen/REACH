How REACH works

From sign-up to sharing in a few minutes. Here is everything step by step.

Create and share your card

1

Create your account

Sign up with your name, email and a password. Then log in to open your dashboard.

2

Add your platforms

Type the name of a platform, like Instagram, WhatsApp, WeChat or LinkedIn, and its logo appears by itself. Then add your username, number or link.

3

Add your photo and details

Upload a profile photo and write your name, job title, a short bio and a longer “About me”. Running a business? Add your company name and logo, address, website, office phone and business WhatsApp.

4

Share your QR code or link

Copy your card link, or download the QR image with your name and the REACH logo. Anyone who scans it sees your card straight away.

Show what you offer (optional)

5

Turn on “What we offer”

Describe your business, add your opening hours and the areas you serve, then add products or services with photos, prices and short descriptions. It works for any business. You can switch it on for any card, personal or business.

6

Let people reach you

Your card gets WhatsApp, Call and Message buttons. Messages arrive in the Inbox on your dashboard, and you reply using the number the visitor left. You can mark a message as spam.

7

Free trial, then simple plans

Turning the section on starts a free 10-day trial with up to 50 items. After the trial, 5 items of your choice stay visible for free, or you can upgrade to REACH Pro. Nothing is deleted. See the Pricing page for the full details.

Print business cards

8

Design your card

In the Print card tab, pick your colour and style and tick what shows on the front. The QR code is on the back. Downloads carry a REACH watermark so you can preview them.

9

Order printed cards

Choose how many cards you want and how you will get them. You pay by bank or mobile money, then upload your receipt. When your payment is approved, the watermark is removed from that card and your cards are printed.

10

Watch it work

Open the Analytics tab to see views, unique visitors, QR scans and which platforms people tap most.

For people who receive a card

👀

Open it instantly

Scan the QR code or tap the link. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

💬

Reach out your way

Tap WhatsApp or Call, send a message, or save the details to your contacts with one tap.
