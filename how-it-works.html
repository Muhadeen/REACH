How REACH works

From sign-up to sharing in a few minutes. Here is everything step by step.

Create and share your card

1

Create your account

Sign up with your name, email and a password. Then log in to open your dashboard.

2

Add your platforms

Type the name of a platform, like Instagram, WhatsApp, WeChat or LinkedIn, and its logo appears by itself. Then add your username, number or link.

3

Add your photo and details

Upload a profile photo and write your name, job title and a short bio. Running a business? Add your company name and logo, address, website, office phone and business WhatsApp.

4

Share your QR code or link

Copy your card link, or download the QR image with your name and the REACH logo. Anyone who scans it sees your card straight away.

5

Print a business card

Pick your colour and style, choose what shows on the front, and download the front and back as print-ready images.

6

Watch it work

Open the Analytics tab to see your views, unique visitors, QR scans and which platforms people tap most.

For people who receive a card

👀

Open it instantly

Scan the QR code or tap the link. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

➕

Save to contacts

Tap “Save to my contacts” to add the name, phone numbers, email and company to your phone in one go.

Good to know

🗂️

More than one card

Use the “New card” button to make a business card, a personal one, or one for a side project.

🔑

Forgot your password?

Use “Forgot password?” on the login screen. A reset link arrives in your email. Need help? Contact us.
