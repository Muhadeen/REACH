“What we offer”: how the trial and plans work

1

Start your free trial

When you turn on “What we offer” for the first time, a free 10-day trial begins. Not before. During the trial you can add up to 50 items.

2

When the trial ends

You keep 5 items visible for free. You choose which ones. Any others are hidden from your public card, never deleted.

3

Or upgrade to REACH Pro

Pro shows up to 50 items again. You pay by bank transfer or mobile money, upload your receipt, and we approve it. Pro lasts for the months you pay for.

4

If Pro runs out

You get 5 more days with everything still visible, so you can renew without losing anything. After that, 5 items stay visible and the rest are hidden, not deleted. They come back as soon as you pay.

Printed business cards

🖨️

Order inside REACH

Choose your pack size and delivery or pickup. The price is shown before you place the order.

💧

About the watermark

Your downloaded card preview has a REACH watermark. It is removed for that card once your printed card order is paid and approved.

How you pay

You pay by bank transfer or mobile money. Each order gets its own reference. After paying, you upload a screenshot of your receipt with the transaction ID. We check that the money arrived, then approve it. You can follow every order and payment in the Orders tab.
