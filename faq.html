Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about creating, sharing and printing your REACH card.

What is REACH?
REACH is a contact storage and sharing platform. You put all your contact details and social platforms on one card, then share it with a link or a QR code.
Is it free?
Yes. Right now you can create an account, build cards, download your QR code and print your business card at no cost.
Do people need an account to view my card?
No. Anyone with your link or QR code can open your card on any phone. They do not need to sign up or install anything.
How do I create my card?
Sign up, log in, then add your platforms and details. Your card is ready as soon as you save. Our How it works page has the full steps.
Which platforms can I add?
Almost any. Type a name and the logo is recognised automatically for hundreds of platforms worldwide, in many languages and scripts, including WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, WeChat, LINE, VK, Telegram, mobile money services and more. If a platform is not recognised, paste its link into the details box and its logo appears. Otherwise you get a neat letter badge.
How does the QR code work?
Your QR code holds the link to your card. When someone scans it with their phone camera, your card opens. You can download the QR as an image with your name and the REACH logo.
Can I have more than one card?
Yes. Use “New card” on your dashboard to add a personal card, a business card or any other. Each card has its own link and QR code.
Can I add my company details?
Yes. On a business card you can add your company name and logo, job title, address, website, office telephone and business WhatsApp.
Can I print a business card?
Yes. Open the Print card tab, choose your colour, pick what appears on the front, then download the front and back as 3.5 × 2 inch print-ready images. The QR code is on the back.
Can I choose the colour of my card?
Yes. Pick from the colour swatches or choose any colour you like, then pick a style: gradient, solid, light or dark. Your online card uses the same colour.
Who can see my details?
Anyone who has your card link or QR code can see what you put on that card. Only share it with people you want to reach you, and remember you can edit or delete a card any time.
What do the analytics show?
For each card you can see total views, unique visitors, QR scans, the platforms people tap most, and views over the last 30 days. Your own visits are not counted.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
On the login screen tap “Forgot password?”, enter your email, and a reset link is sent to you. Check your spam or junk folder too. If it still does not arrive, contact us.
I lost my QR code. Can I get it again?
Yes. Log in, open your card and download the QR image again. Your QR code never changes for a card. If you cannot log in, contact us and we can help you get it.
How do I delete a card or my account?
Open the Details tab. You can delete a single card there, or delete your whole account at the bottom. Deleting a card makes its QR code stop working.
Is my password safe?
Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, so nobody at REACH can see them.
I have an idea or found a problem.
We would love to hear it. Use the “Give feedback” link at the bottom of the home page, or visit our Contact page.
